# What we've tested

{: .in-short }
What our experiments found, in plain numbers, and what they don't yet show.

{: .outline }
This page is an outline. The headings and points below show what it will cover.

## Describing material

- The same lesson gives a consistent description.

## Matching to the right lesson

- About three times in four by closeness alone; nearly nine in ten with the classifier.

## When the curriculum changes

- Renamed and split lessons are still found; merged and removed ones are harder.

## Telling easier from harder

- The description can tell which version of a lesson is harder, with a caveat.

## Linking two curricula

- Matching the same lessons to two curricula shows which parts correspond.

## How much data a year makes

- Storage is small; reading it back the way the proof of concept does is the problem.

## What we haven't tested

- Real school files, a real second curriculum, real tools' data, and whether any of this improves tutoring.
