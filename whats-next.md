# What's next

{: .in-short }
Beyond the proof of concept, we've split the next stage into six areas, so each can be designed and built alongside the others.

## The six areas

1. **Connecting to real systems.**
   Schools' pupil records systems and wherever they keep their materials, a real learning record store, the tutoring tools themselves, and where the whole thing is hosted.
2. **Standards and rules.**
   The shape of each record, the words records can use, the rules a tool must follow, and who is allowed to read or write what.
3. **The integration layer's interface.**
   The functions tools can call, what classroom context contains, and how the functions change over time without breaking anyone.
4. **Curricula.**
   Which curriculum map we build on, keeping its references stable as it changes, links between lessons, and lining up other organisations' maps with ours.
5. **AI judgements.**
   Describing materials, matching them to lessons, building each pupil's summary, and checking that all of these are good enough to rely on.
6. **Learning records.**
   Where records are kept and who controls them, how long they're kept, and how they're corrected, deleted or handed on.

## What we'll test next

- Real school files, scanned or in mixed formats.
- A curriculum map built by someone other than Oak.
- Records from a real tutoring tool.
- How much it costs, and how fast it is, at the size of a real school or trust.
